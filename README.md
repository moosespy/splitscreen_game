This is a two-player game that is genuinely addictive, at least for me! It's a simple premise - you and another player on the same device fight each other with projectiles. Each player has a certain amount of health, and once a player loses all their health, then it's game over for them and the other player wins!

At the beginning of the game, each player chooses a character from a preset list of three different characters. Each character has different speed, damage, and special attack attributes. Once each player has chosen a character, the game starts! Each player spawns on opposite sides of a randomly generated map, and falling projectiles will start appearing at the top of your screen. As the game goes on, the falling projectiles will continue to fall faster and faster, eventually forcing a conclusion to the battle if it really drags on for too long.

# Movement
Players move by going left and right, and jumping.

# Attacks
Every player shares a basic attack, which is just a projectile that moves linearly, horizontally across the screen until it hits a despawn limit or a platform or another player. 

Every player also has their own unique attack, allowing them to try different things against different players! 

# Controls
Player 1 uses arrow keys to move, 'o' for basic attack, and 'p' for special attack. Player 2 uses wasd to move, '1' for basic attack, and '2' for special attack.
